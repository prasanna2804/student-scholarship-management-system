About Scholarships

Scholarships provide financial support to students based on merit, category, or financial need. We propose the implementation of a scholarship program aimed at supporting talented students from low-income families. This initiative seeks to bridge the gap between financial limitations and educational opportunities, enabling deserving students to pursue their academic aspirations and unlock their potential. Scholarships will be awarded to selected students to cover their tuition fees, educational materials, transportation costs, and other necessary expenses. The scholarship amount will be determined based on the financial needs of the students and the educational institution's fee structure.

Scholarship Schemes

  • Post-Matric Scholarship:

    • For students pursuing higher studies beyond 10th grade. Should be pursuing post-matric courses in recognized institutions. The annual parental income should not exceed ₹2,50,000. Students can apply through their respective educational institutions.

  • SC Scholarship:

    • Students must belong to Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, or Adi Dravidar communities. Must be residents of Tamil Nadu. Should be pursuing post-matric courses in recognized institutions. The annual parental income should not exceed ₹2,50,000.

  • BC & MBC Scholarship:

    • For students belonging to Backward & Most Backward Classes.Must be residents of Tamil Nadu. Should be studying in recognized post-matric courses (i.e., courses beyond the 10th standard). For BC students, the annual parental income should not exceed ₹2,50,000. There is no income limit for MBC and DNC students
